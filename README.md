## Research and project areas

### AI governance and data assurance

Research and engineering focused on accountable AI systems, cross-database transparency, data provenance, independent reconciliation, and human-controlled release.

The QT Superintelligence cross-database transparency project is maintained in a private repository. Access is restricted; this public portfolio does not expose private source code or internal project details.

### Energy infrastructure transparency

[ESRIT — Energy Sovereign Rights & Infrastructure Transparency](https://github.com/DivineVirtuousChief/Energy-transfer) explores evidence-neutral, audit-first governance for utility charges, infrastructure investment, operational performance, consumer protections, and regulatory accountability.

### Civic and public-interest technology

Additional work explores technology-enabled civic accountability and access to justice. Some related development repositories are private.

## Working principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Human accountability for consequential decisions.
- Privacy, security, and least privilege by design.
- Clear separation between proposed policy, implemented controls, and independently verified outcomes.
- Transparent limitations and reproducible evidence.
